🔔 The Bell Echoes

**An Immersive 3D Horror Experience — Where Unity Meets the Echoes of Fear**


## 🎮 About the Game

*The Bell Echoes* is a first-person psychological horror game set inside a cursed, abandoned school.

You play as a young girl who suddenly finds herself completely alone…
No classmates. No teachers. Just silence.

Then the first bell rings.

The school begins to change.

## ⏳ The Eight Bell System

With every bell that echoes through the halls:

* The environment distorts
* Shadows begin to move
* Reality starts to break

You have **eight bells** to uncover the truth… and escape.
If the final bell rings—there’s no way out.

## 🏫 Core Experience

* Atmospheric horror exploration
* Psychological tension and environmental storytelling

## ⚙️ Built With

* Unity (Game Engine)
* C# (Game Logic)
* Blender (3D Assets & Environment Design)
